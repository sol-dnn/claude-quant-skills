## Rules

- Do not invent decisions.
- Mark uncertain points as "to confirm".
- Separate technical tasks from business / market questions.
- Make action items concrete and ordered by priority.
- Keep the output practical.
- If the meeting involved a PM, focus on the business implication and next decision.
- If the meeting involved a quant mentor or developer, focus on execution, technical clarity, and priorities.
- Distinguish between what was explicitly decided and what is only a possible next step.
